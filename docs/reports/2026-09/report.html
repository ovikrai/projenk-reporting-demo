Data quality & methodology

The complete input contains 832 rows: 805 accepted, 27 rejected and 58 normalized. Metrics above cover only 2026-09. Duplicate IDs retain the first valid row; invalid records are excluded and recorded.

Revenue = sum of completed orders × quantity × unit price × (1 − discount / 100), rounded per order to cents. Gross profit = revenue − completed-order cost of goods. Margin = gross profit / revenue. AOV = revenue / completed orders. Refund rate = refunded / (completed + refunded). Refunded and cancelled orders contribute no revenue or cost. Shipping, tax, operating expenses and partial refunds are outside this demo.
